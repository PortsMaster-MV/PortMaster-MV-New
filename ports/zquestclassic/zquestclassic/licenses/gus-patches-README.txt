The MIDI instrument patches in midi/ are the original Gravis UltraSound
GUS patch set, taken unchanged from the ZQuestClassic repository
(timidity/ultra/), where upstream redistributes them for its web player.

midi/default.cfg is a DIGMID index generated from upstream's
timidity/ultra.cfg instrument mapping.

The patches carry no license file of their own; copyright belongs to
their original authors (Advanced Gravis Computer Technology).
