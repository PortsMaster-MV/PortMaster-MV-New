The portable ScaleFX edge-smoothing compute shader is compiled into
starfox.aarch64 as part of the Star Fox Enhanced runtime, derived from
Sp00kyFox's ScaleFX passes 0-4 in libretro/slang-shaders. This prototype's
shipped pregame.cfg disables the effects that use it (ANTI_ALIASING,
ENHANCED_GRAPHICS, TWO_D_FILTER), but the shader is present in the binary.

Source: https://github.com/libretro/slang-shaders/tree/b61e1ee4fc9e2119ec933461a0bfad024dd2950a/edge-smoothing/scalefx
