Put your own Star Fox / Starwing ROM in this folder.

  - File type: .sfc or .smc (a 512-byte copier header is fine)
  - Unmodified retail dump, USA, Japan or Europe (see README.md for the
    supported revisions and CRC32 checksums)
  - Any file name works, e.g. "Star Fox (USA).sfc"

On the first launch the game builds Starfox-Assets.BIN from the ROM.

No ROM is included with this port and none will be provided.
